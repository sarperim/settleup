# Domain Map — Settle Up

Derived from `.pipeline/00-project-brief.md`. The brief scopes the MVP as one continuous build effort with all scope-in capabilities required, so every domain below is **Must** for this release. Scope-out items (notifications, multi-currency, member removal, group deletion, self-serve password reset, non-equal/exact splits, etc.) are excluded from every domain and recorded as "Won't — this release" in the relevant domain reports where they need a explicit boundary.

**Assumption status at time of writing:** ASM-001 (remainder-cent rule on uneven equal splits) is **decided** — the random-spread rule: each participant's base share is amount ÷ participant count rounded down to the kuruş, and leftover kuruş are assigned one each to randomly chosen distinct participants (no participant more than one extra kuruş), drawn once at expense creation and stored with the expense. The brief was amended accordingly on 2026-09-25 by user decision. ASM-002 (TRY, 2 decimals), ASM-003 (current password required to change password), and ASM-004 (standard session management incl. logout) are stated in the brief and carried into the reports; their validation is scheduled for architecture/security review per the brief.

## Decomposition notes

- **Balances and Settlement are one domain, not two.** Settlement suggestions are computed from balances, and marking a payment paid (or undoing it) adjusts balances — SC-005 names settle/undo among the operations that must preserve the zero-sum. Splitting them would create a circular dependency: Settlement would need Balances to compute suggestions, while Balances would need Settlement Records to compute net positions. One domain owns the entire net-and-settle capability.
- **Privacy (OBJ-005) is not a domain.** It is a cross-cutting business rule. Groups & Membership owns the membership record (the source of truth for who may see what), and the rule is restated as authorization requirements in every domain that exposes group-scoped data (Expense Tracking, Balances & Settlement).
- **Every entity has exactly one owning domain.** Expense Share (the per-participant split result, including remainder-cent assignment) is owned by Expense Tracking; Member Balance is owned by Balances & Settlement and is derived from Expense Shares plus Settlement Records. No entity appears in two domains.
- **Manual password reset is part of Accounts & Access, not its own domain.** It is an owner-operated procedure on the User Account record (brief §4, §6 scope-out), far too small to be a coherent business capability on its own.
- **No Should/Could/Won't domains.** All in-scope capabilities are required for the MVP release. Future-release candidates from the scope-out list are captured in each domain report's Open Questions where relevant.
